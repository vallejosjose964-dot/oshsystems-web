03 · Progress & evolution

From software research to physical execution.

The development path matters because each stage removed a different alternative explanation: software-only behavior, timing ambiguity, portability assumptions and finally the absence of a real physical feedback loop.

01

PC Resident Runtime

Resident execution and measurable state transitions on CPU.

02

Benchmark 3

03

Cross-target portability

Compile PASS across ARM Cortex-M, RISC-V, AVR and MIPS32 profiles.

04

Native ESP32-S3

Physical MCU execution on silicon, not emulation.

05

Causal parity

Two physical units and 150/150 checks against the frozen PC reference.

06

Physical closed loop

100,000 cycles through external analog feedback with external timing evidence.

Portability results for ARM Cortex-M, RISC-V, AVR and MIPS32 are compiler-level evidence only. Physical-board validation in the current public paper is restricted to ESP32-S3.

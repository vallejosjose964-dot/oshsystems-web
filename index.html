06 · Physical validation paper

External timing. Endurance. Causal parity. Real analog feedback.

The physical paper carries OSH from host execution through native ESP32-S3 execution, cross-device reproduction, five-level PC–MCU causal parity, long-run endurance, oscilloscope instrumentation and a final analog feedback experiment.

In the final route, a physical analog reference enters through ADC, OSH computes locally, the response leaves the MCU as PWM, passes through an external RC network and returns through a different ADC input.

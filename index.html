OSH in 30 seconds

A machine with an internal state that can be perturbed, regulated and carried forward.

For a non-technical visitor, the simplest way to understand OSH is this: information does not merely trigger a prediction. It changes an internal configuration. The system then regulates that change and produces its next action from the state it has reached.

Not a larger model.
A different computation.
01

Sense

External information perturbs the current internal configuration.

02

Deform

The active state changes according to the incoming condition and the system's continuity.

03

Regulate

OSH settles toward a coherent operating region instead of launching a next-token prediction loop.

04

Act

Language, control or another output becomes a discharge from the regulated state.

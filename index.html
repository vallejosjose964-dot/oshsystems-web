A new architecture for intelligence inside the machine.

OSH is a hardware-bound homeostatic computing architecture. Instead of relying on a Transformer to continuously predict the next token, OSH maintains a compact internal state, reacts to perturbation, regulates that state and produces output from the configuration it reaches.

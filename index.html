OSH in plain language

Intelligence that regulates itself instead of continuously predicting the next token.

Think of OSH as a compact computational organism. Information changes its internal state. The system evaluates that change, settles into a regulated configuration, preserves continuity, and produces an action or semantic output from that state.

The point is not to make a larger model.
The point is to change the computation.
01

Sense

External information enters as a perturbation to the current internal configuration.

02

Deform

The internal state changes geometrically and functionally instead of launching next-token prediction.

03

Regulate

OSH settles the system toward a coherent operating region while preserving continuity.

04

Act

Language, control or another output becomes a discharge from the regulated state.

For everyone

What is it?

A different way of building machine intelligence: local, stateful and self-regulating, designed to remain inside the device instead of depending on a remote model for every decision.

For business

Why could it matter?

If target workloads validate, a compact local runtime can change where intelligence lives, what hardware is required, how products are licensed and how much operational control stays with the owner.

For engineers

What is measurable?

Native MCU execution, parity against a frozen PC reference, numerical error, memory, endurance, deterministic outputs, artifact hashes and target-specific integration constraints.

Business strategy

OSH is being built as a protected computational substrate — not as one application.

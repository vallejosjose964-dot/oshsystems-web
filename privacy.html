Privacy & cookies

Last updated: 8 November 2026.

This notice covers the public OSH Global Systems website at oshsystems.com. OSH Global Systems, Inc. is responsible for the website and its use of analytics. For privacy questions or requests, contact fabian.vallejos@oshsystems.com.

Your choice comes first

Google Analytics is optional and requires your consent. Before you accept, this website does not load the Google Analytics tag or send analytics requests, including cookieless analytics pings. Rejecting analytics does not limit access to the website, reports, or downloads. This privacy page does not run analytics.

Use “Cookie settings” in the home page footer to accept, reject, or withdraw consent. Withdrawal stops future collection, removes this site's accessible Google Analytics cookies, and reloads the home page to remove the running tag. It does not undo data already sent. You can also remove website data in your browser. Without JavaScript, analytics does not run.

What optional analytics measures

If you accept, OSH uses Google Analytics 4, provided by Google, to measure visits. The reports can include approximate visitor and session counts, device and browser information, and approximate geographic information. These counts are estimates and exclude visitors who reject analytics, block tracking, or disable JavaScript. There is no public visitor counter.

The tag uses pseudonymous cookie identifiers. Google receives network and device information needed for analytics, including the IP address used by the connection. Google states that GA4 does not log or store individual IP addresses and uses them to derive approximate location. Analytics should not be treated as anonymous or as a way to identify named visitors.

The website sends a fixed public page address and title, without tracking downloads or clicks. It does not send contact-form fields, names, email addresses, page query strings or fragments, or referring-page URLs to analytics. Google Signals, advertising storage, ad-personalization signals, and advertising user-data consent are disabled in the tag. Analytics is not used here for advertising.

Cookies and browser storage

  • Consent preference: the browser storage entry “osh_analytics_consent_v1” records your choice and its expiry for up to 180 days. It stays in your browser and is not sent to Google by this site. It is used to remember your privacy choice.
  • Optional analytics cookies: “_ga” and “_ga_9TLWQDVEFQ” can distinguish browsers and sessions after acceptance. This site configures them to expire within 180 days without automatically extending their lifetime. Browser restrictions may shorten that period.

Consent is requested again when the saved preference expires or is removed. If browser storage is unavailable, a choice may apply only to the current page.

Recipients, transfers, and retention

Analytics reports are intended for OSH's authorized account users, not website visitors. Google processes analytics information under its applicable service terms and data-processing terms. Processing may occur outside your country, including in the United States. Applicable transfer safeguards depend on Google's terms and the laws that apply to you.

The cookie lifetime above is separate from Google's server-side retention. GA4 event and user data follow the retention settings of the OSH property; aggregated reports may be retained longer. OSH should keep analytics only as long as necessary for the stated purpose and applicable obligations. Contact OSH for the current retention setting or a request concerning data already collected.

See Google's Privacy Policy, GA4 privacy and IP-address information, and Google's analytics browser opt-out tool.

Hosting and contact messages

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Hosting providers necessarily receive connection details to deliver pages and files and may process operational or security logs under their own policies. This is separate from optional Google Analytics. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The contact form prepares a draft in your email application. The form itself does not submit information to an OSH server or to Google Analytics. If you send the email, OSH and the email services involved receive the information you choose to include for responding to your inquiry and managing the resulting relationship.

Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict, or receive your personal data, object to certain processing, withdraw consent, and complain to a competent privacy authority. Contact OSH using the address above. Please avoid sending sensitive information unnecessarily. OSH may need sufficient information to verify and locate a request, and browser analytics data may not identify you by name.
